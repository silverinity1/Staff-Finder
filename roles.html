Sales Agent Information

Sales Agents earn a percentage from successful orders made by clients they bring in.

Payment Method Payments are made through Apple Pay or gift cards.
Commission Earn 15–20% from each person you bring in who places an order.
Cash Out You may cash out once you reach $5 worth of sales.

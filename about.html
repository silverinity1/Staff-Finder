About Staff Finder

Built for the your community.

Staff Finder is a community focused on connecting potential staff, server owners, creators, and people looking for opportunities.

What is Staff Finder?

A place where communities and players can meet, connect, and find new opportunities.

Our Community
Staff Finder brings together people from different parts of different places. Whether you're looking for staff opportunities, promoting a server, meeting new players, or connecting with other communities, Staff Finder provides a place to get started.

What You Can Find Here

Staff Finder offers different ways to get involved.

Staff Opportunities Find roles and opportunities to become involved within communities and Staff Finder.
Server Promotion Connect with server owners and explore opportunities to promote communities.
Player Networking Meet people, discover communities, and build new connections.
Community Services Access services designed to help communities grow and reach more people.

What We Value

Community
